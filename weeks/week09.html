10열십

📌 어느 것을 쓸까 · Which to use

Native (하나, 둘…): hours, age, counting objects (시, 살, 개, 명).
Sino (일, 이…): minutes, money, dates, phone numbers (분, 원, 월, 일).
Time mixes both: 세 시 삼십 분 = 3:30 (Native hour + Sino minute).

❓ 질문 만들기 · Making questions

Just raise your pitch: 먹어요? (Do you eat?). Add a question word where the answer goes: 뭐 먹어요? (What do you eat?) · 어디 가요? (Where do you go?)

🎭 종합 대화 · A full conversation

함께 Read aloud — this uses everything!
A
안녕하세요! 오랜만이에요. 잘 지냈어요?
annyeonghaseyo! oraenmanieyo. jal jinaesseoyo?
Hello! Long time no see. How have you been?
B
네, 잘 지냈어요! 어제 친구를 만났어요.
ne, jal jinaesseoyo! eoje chingureul mannasseoyo.
Yes, I've been well! I met a friend yesterday.
A
어디에서 만났어요? 뭐 했어요?
eodi-eseo mannasseoyo? mwo haesseoyo?
Where did you meet? What did you do?
B
카페에서 만났어요. 같이 커피를 마셨어요.
kape-eseo mannasseoyo. gachi keopireul masyeosseoyo.
We met at a café. We drank coffee together.
A
좋았어요? 우리도 언제 만나요!
joasseoyo? urido eonje mannayo!
Was it nice? Let's meet sometime too!
B
네, 좋아요! 이번 주말 어때요?
ne, joayo! ibeon jumal eottaeyo?
Yes, great! How about this weekend?
