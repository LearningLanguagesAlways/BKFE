환영합니다! Welcome Back

잘 돌아오셨어요! 한글을 읽을 수 있으니, 이제 숫자로 넘어가요. Korean has not one but two number systems — and knowing which one to use unlocks time, dates, counting, and so much more.

이번 주 목표 · This week you will…

  • 🔢 Count 1–100 in both Native Korean & Sino-Korean numbers.
  • ⏰ Tell the time — hours (Native), minutes (Sino), AM/PM, noon & midnight.
  • 📅 Say days, months & full dates — including today's date.
  • 🗓️ Make and respond to an appointment in natural Korean.
  • 🗣️ Learn ~35 real-world words tied to time & daily life.

두 가지 숫자 체계 · Why two systems?

Korean uses Native Korean numbers (순우리말 수) for hours, ages, counting objects, and floors. It uses Sino-Korean numbers (한자어 수) — borrowed from Chinese — for minutes, money, dates, phone numbers, and years. You'll know which to use by context — and this week you'll see exactly how.

순우리말 수 Native Korean Numbers

함께 Teacher-led혼자 Independent
When to use 순우리말 수: hours on the clock · ages · counting objects (with counters like 개, 명, 살) · floors in a building

1–10

11–99 — how it works

Numbers combine simply: 열 (10) + 하나 (1) = 열하나 (11). Just say the tens, then the ones.

KoreanNumberExample expression
스물20스물하나 (21)
서른30서른둘 (32)
마흔40마흔셋 (43)
쉰50쉰다섯 (55)
예순60예순일곱 (67)
일흔70일흔여덟 (78)
여든80여든아홉 (89)
아흔90아흔 (90)
⚠️ 중요! Before a counter, 하나→한, 둘→두, 셋→세, 넷→네.
사과 한 개 (one apple) — NOT 하나 개. 사람 두 명 (two people) — NOT 둘 명.

11–100 — the pattern

Sino-Korean is delightfully regular: 십 (10) + 일 (1) = 십일 (11). Just place the tens digit before 십, then add ones.

KoreanNumber
이십 (二十)20
삼십 (三十)30
사십 (四十)40
오십 (五十)50
백 (百)100
천 (千)1,000
만 (萬)10,000

Example: 삼십칠 = 37  |  구십구 = 99  |  이백 = 200

시간 Telling Time

함께 Teacher-led
The rule: Hours → Native Korean + 시  |  Minutes → Sino-Korean + 분
AM = 오전 (午前)  |  PM = 오후 (午後)  |  Noon = 정오  |  Midnight = 자정

🌏 문화 팁 · Culture note — the five elements
Korean weekday names come from the five Taoist elements (오행) plus sun and moon — the same system used in Japan and China. 금요일 (Gold/Metal) = Friday, 수요일 (Water) = Wednesday.

⚠️ 예외 · Irregular: 6월 = 유월 (not 육월) · 10월 = 시월 (not 십월) — these are spoken contractions.

일 Day numbers + full date formula

Formula: [Year]년 [Month]월 [Day]일

Example: 이천이십육년 유월 팔일 = June 8, 2026

Excuse me, what time is it now?

ohu du si ban-ieyo.
It's 2:30 PM.

gamsahamnida!
Thank you!

🎭 모델 대화 2 · Making an appointment

이번 주 금요일에 시간 있어요?
ibeon ju geumyoile sigan isseoyo?
Do you have time this Friday?
네, 있어요! 몇 시에 만날까요?
ne, isseoyo! myeot sie mannalkkayo?
Yes, I'm free! What time shall we meet?
오후 세 시 어때요?
ohu se si eottaeyo?
How about 3 PM?
좋아요! 그럼 금요일 오후 세 시에 봐요.
joayo! geureom geumyoil ohu se sie bwayo.
Great! Then let's meet Friday at 3 PM.
네, 그때 봐요. 화이팅!
ne, geuttae bwayo. hwaiting!
Yes, see you then. You got this!
