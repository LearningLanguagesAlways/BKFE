이번 주 목표 · This week you will…

  • 🔢 Count 1–99 in both Native Korean and Sino-Korean number systems.
  • ⏰ Tell the time — hours (Native), minutes (Sino), AM/PM, noon & midnight.
  • 📅 Say days of the week, months & full dates — including today's date in Korean.
  • 🗓️ Make and accept an appointment using natural Korean.
  • 🗣️ Learn ~35 real-world words for daily schedules and life.

두 가지 숫자 체계 · Why does Korean have TWO number systems?

Korean borrowed one set of numbers from Chinese (한자어 수 · Sino-Korean) for minutes, money, dates, and phone numbers. It kept its own ancient set (순우리말 수 · Native Korean) for hours, ages, and counting objects. By the end of today you'll know exactly which to use — and it becomes automatic fast!

⚠️ 중요! Counter shortening rule — 조수사 앞에서 줄어요

Before a counter word, some numbers shorten:
하나 → 한  |  둘 → 두  |  셋 → 세  |  넷 → 네
예: 사과 한 개 (one apple) ✓    NOT: 사과 하나 개 ✗

🌏 문화 노트 · Culture note — the Five Elements

Korean weekday names come from the five Taoist cosmic elements (오행) plus the Sun and Moon — the same system used in Japan and China. 금요일 (Gold day) = Friday. 수요일 (Water day) = Wednesday. Once you know the Hanja, you never forget the days!

⚠️ 예외 두 개 · Two irregular months: 6월 = 유월 (not 육월) · 10월 = 시월 (not 십월) — spoken contractions.

Example: 이천이십육년 유월 팔일 = June 8, 2026
