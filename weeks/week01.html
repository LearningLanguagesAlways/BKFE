알아두면 좋아요 · Good to know — why Hangul first?

Korean and English sounds don't line up one-to-one, so romanization (English spelling) is only a rough guide. Once you can read Hangul, your pronunciation and everything that follows gets much easier. Hangul was created in 1443 to be simple to learn — you can read it sooner than you think!

잠깐! · The silent friend ㅇ

ㅇ is a placeholder. At the start of a syllable it makes no sound (it just holds the vowel's spot), so 아 = "a". At the end of a syllable it sounds like "ng" — 강 = "gang".

모양의 비밀 · The shape secret

Vertical vowels (ㅏ ㅓ ㅣ) and horizontal vowels (ㅗ ㅜ ㅡ) are built from a single line plus a short stroke. The direction of that little stroke tells you the sound — a clever, logical system.

글자 만들기 Building Syllables

Korean is written in neat syllable blocks, not in a straight line. Every block = a consonant + a vowel (and sometimes a final consonant).

두 가지 규칙 · Two simple rules

① Vertical vowels (ㅏ ㅓ ㅣ) go to the right of the consonant:  ㄴ + ㅏ → 나 (na)

② Horizontal vowels (ㅗ ㅜ ㅡ) go underneath:  ㄷ + ㅗ → 도 (do)

Need a vowel-only sound? Use silent ㅇ as the placeholder: ㅇ + ㅏ → 아 (a).

자기소개 Introduce Yourself

A Korean self-introduction is just four friendly lines: greet → name → where you're from → nice to meet you. Let's learn each piece.

핵심 표현 · Key phrases
한국어 Korean로마자 RomanizationEnglish
안녕하세요annyeonghaseyoHello (polite, all-purpose) — give a small bow
안녕annyeongHi (casual, with friends)
제 이름은 ___이에요/예요je ireumeun ___ ieyo / yeyoMy name is ___ (polite)
저는 ___입니다jeoneun ___ imnidaI am ___ (formal)
저는 ___에서 왔어요jeoneun ___-eseo wasseoyoI'm from ___
만나서 반갑습니다mannaseo bangapseumnidaNice to meet you (polite / formal)
잘 부탁드립니다jal butak-deurimnida"I look forward to it / please treat me well"

중요한 규칙 · The 이에요 / 예요 rule

For "My name is ___," choose the ending by the last sound of your name:

• Ends in a consonant → 이에요.   예: 수진 → 수진이에요 (Sujin-ieyo)
• Ends in a vowel → 예요.   예: 미나 → 미나예요 (Mina-yeyo)

팁 · When unsure, the polite 요-form is friendly and correct in almost every real-life situation.

문화 노트 · Culture note — first impressions

🙇 Bow slightly (a 15° nod) as you say 안녕하세요. 🤝 If shaking hands, use your right hand with your left hand lightly supporting your forearm. 🙌 Give and receive things (like a business card) with both hands. 🗣️ Address people by name + 씨 (ssi) — e.g., 민수 씨 — rather than first name alone.
