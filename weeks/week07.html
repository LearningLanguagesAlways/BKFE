오늘의 큰 그림 · The big idea today

The past tense reuses everything you learned in Week 5! Take the same vowel rule, insert 았 or 었, and finish with 어요. 먹다 → 먹었어요 (ate), 가다 → 갔어요 (went). If you know -아요/-어요, you already know 90% of this.

과거란 What Is the Past Tense?

현재형에서 살짝만 바꾸면 과거형이 돼요.

함께 Teacher-led

📌 핵심 · Present becomes past — insert 았/었

Present먹어요
→
Past먹었어요

"(I) eat" → "(I) ate" — the 었 carries the past meaning.

현재 → 과거 · Present → Past
PresentPastEnglish
가요갔어요go → went
먹어요먹었어요eat → ate
해요했어요do → did
마셔요마셨어요drink → drank

💡 좋은 소식 · Good news

Korean has just one past tense — no "ate / have eaten / was eating" confusion like English! 먹었어요 covers them all. Context (and time words like 어제) tells the listener exactly when.

3

규칙 The -았어요 / -었어요 Rule

Week 5와 똑같은 모음 규칙이에요 — 그래서 쉬워요!

함께 Teacher-led혼자 Independent
-았어요
If the last stem vowel is ㅏ or ㅗ
살다 → 살았어요 · 좋다 → 좋았어요
-었어요
All other vowels (ㅓ, ㅜ, ㅣ, ㅡ…)
먹다 → 먹었어요 · 읽다 → 읽었어요
했어요
If the verb ends in 하다
공부하다 → 공부했어요

🎵 기억법 · It's the same rule as Week 5

Same vowel choice as the present tense — just add ㅆ어요. -아요 becomes -았어요, -어요 becomes -었어요, 해요 becomes 했어요. If you know the present, the past is almost automatic!

소리 합치기 · When vowels merge

DictionaryBecomes (past)English
가다갔어요went
오다왔어요came
보다봤어요saw / watched
마시다마셨어요drank
주다줬어요gave

💡 이에요/예요의 과거 · The past of "to be"

이에요/예요 → 이었어요/였어요. "학생이에요" (I'm a student) → "학생이었어요" (I was a student). "친구예요" → "친구였어요" (was a friend). Very common — listen for it!

📌 문장 속에서 · In a sentence

Time words go near the front: 어제 영화를 봤어요. (Yesterday I watched a movie.) · 지난주에 친구를 만났어요. (Last week I met a friend.)

B · 수진
