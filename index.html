학습 방법 · How we learn

A method built for adult beginners

읽기 먼저 · Read first

Korean and English sounds don't match one-to-one, so we start with Hangul. Once you can read, every later lesson becomes easier — and far more empowering.

소리 내어 · Out loud

Say each letter, word, and line aloud. Listening + speaking is how pronunciation sticks. Every exercise is meant to be spoken, not just read.

조금씩 매일 · A little daily

Short, repeated practice beats cramming. Each week reviews the last so vocabulary moves into long-term memory through gentle spaced repetition.

실생활 어휘 · Real words

You'll learn words people actually use today — greetings, family, food, and survival phrases — so you can use Korean from day one.

👩‍🏫 Teacher-supervised & independent practice · 함께, 그리고 혼자

Activities are labelled so you always know the mode: 함께 Teacher-led for guided practice with your instructor, and 혼자 Independent for solo work — including “create your own” tasks where you build the examples.
